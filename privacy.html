Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026
At Frameora, your privacy matters. Frameora is designed around local media capture and management. Your photos, videos, and audio recordings are stored on your device. We do not require an account, and the core features of the app do not require uploading your personal media to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

Frameora does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use its core features.

Frameora does not operate its own cloud service for storing your captured photos, videos, or audio recordings.

The app processes certain information locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Photos and videos: Media captured using your device's cameras or selected through supported photo library features.
  • Audio recordings: Audio captured through the microphone when the relevant recording feature is used.
  • Capture information: Recording-related information used to support capture quality checks, media review, and organization.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences saved locally to maintain your app experience.

2. Camera and Microphone Permissions

Frameora requires camera access to capture photos and videos using your device's front-facing and rear-facing cameras.

Microphone access is used when audio recording is supported and enabled for a capture workflow.

These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding features. You can manage or revoke camera and microphone permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

3. Photo Library Access

Frameora may request photo library access when you use features that save, access, or export media to your device's photo library.

Your photo library content is not automatically uploaded to Frameora servers. You remain in control of which media you choose to access or export.

You can manage photo library permissions through your device's Settings.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

Frameora is designed for local media storage. Photos, videos, audio recordings, and related capture files are stored on your device.

The app does not require a Frameora account, cloud synchronization, or remote media storage to use its core functionality.

Your media remains under your control. Removing the app or deleting its local data may permanently remove media stored within the app. Please export or back up important recordings before deleting the app.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Frameora does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your locally stored photos, videos, or audio recordings with third parties through a Frameora-operated cloud service.

When you choose to export or share media using iOS system features or other apps, the media you select may be transferred to the destination you choose. That transfer is subject to the destination service's own privacy practices.

6. Tracking and Advertising

Frameora does not use your personal media for advertising or behavioral tracking.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. The app's core capture and media management functionality does not depend on advertising profiles or cross-app tracking.

7. In-App Purchases

Frameora may offer optional paid features or subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Purchases are processed by Apple. Frameora does not receive or store your full payment card information. Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

You can manage eligible subscriptions and purchases through your Apple Account settings.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Media and app preferences are retained locally for as long as they remain on your device.

You can delete individual media items through the app's available controls. You can also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app, subject to any copies you have separately exported or backed up.

Since Frameora does not maintain a cloud account containing your captured media, there is no Frameora cloud media account that you need to request deletion for.

9. Children's Privacy

Frameora does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account registration or cloud data collection system.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of camera, microphone, photo library, and sharing features where appropriate.

10. Security

Frameora is designed to minimize the handling of personal data by keeping core media processing and storage on your device.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including its passcode and operating system updates.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
