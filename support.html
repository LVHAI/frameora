Frequently asked questions

Why are some capture options unavailable?

Available camera combinations and recording capabilities depend on your device hardware, iOS version, and current system conditions. Check the in-app preflight information for details.

Where can I find my captured moments?

Open the Moments or library area in Frameora to review content saved by the app.

Where can I find information about privacy?

Please visit our Privacy Policy page. The full policy will be published there before public release.

Before you contact us

Make sure Frameora is updated to the latest available version. If an issue continues, contact support with the information listed above.
